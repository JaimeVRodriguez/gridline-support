Gridline

Every session. Every season.

Gridline is a dense, fast timing-tower companion for Formula 1 fans on iPhone. Browse results for every race weekend back to 1950, watch a race replay lap by lap with safety cars and pit stops marked, dig into driver and season stats, follow the standings, and run what-if season simulations. Next-race and standings widgets keep you up to date from the Home Screen.

Gridline is free, with no ads and no paywall. Supporters get a few extras and help keep it going.
