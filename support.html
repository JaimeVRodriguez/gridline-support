Support

Common questions

Where does the race data come from?

Results, calendars and standings from 1950 onward come from the community-run Jolpica-F1 API. Practice sessions, tyre stints and safety car, virtual safety car and red flag periods come from OpenF1, which covers 2023 onward. Gridline is not affiliated with either service.

Why does an older race have no safety car markers or tyre data?

Those come from OpenF1, which starts in 2023. Older races still show lap-by-lap positions (from 1996) and pit stops (from 2011) where they exist, with a note that flag data isn't available.

A result or lap looks wrong. What can I do?

Gridline shows the data as the sources publish it, and historical records are occasionally incomplete or corrected later. Pull down to refresh first. If it still looks wrong, email me the race and session and I'll look into it.

What do supporters get?

Gridline is free and nothing is locked behind a purchase. Supporters get alternate app icons, a supporter badge and early access to new features. You can become a supporter for life with a one-time purchase, or with a yearly subscription. The tip jar is separate: tips are one-off thank-yous and don't unlock anything.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Support Gridline and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?

Open Settings > your name > Subscriptions, choose Gridline and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Supporter until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I request a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.

How do I delete my data?

Gridline keeps everything on your iPhone: cached race data and your saved simulator scenarios. Deleting the app removes it all.

Season simulations and projections are what-if estimates for fun, not predictions or advice.
