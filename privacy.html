Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Gridline ("Gridline") is made by Jaime Rodriguez. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: Gridline does not collect your data.

Information we collect

None. Gridline has no accounts and no sign-in. I don't run any servers that receive information about you or how you use the app, and I can't see it.

Where your data lives

Everything Gridline stores stays on your iPhone: cached race data so the app works faster and offline, your saved season-simulator scenarios, your preferences, and whether you're a supporter. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Race data and third-party services

To show race information, Gridline (and its Home Screen widgets) request public data directly from two community-run services: Jolpica-F1 (api.jolpi.ca) and OpenF1 (api.openf1.org). Like any internet request, these requests reveal your device's IP address and basic technical details to those services. Gridline sends them no account, identifier or personal information, and I don't receive anything from these requests. Each service has its own practices, which are described by the service.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

Gridline contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no third-party tracking. It doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Supporter options and tips are purchases handled by the App Store. Apple processes your payment and I don't receive your payment details or your identity. Gridline learns only, on your device, which purchases you own.

Children

Gridline is not directed to children. Because it doesn't collect personal information, it holds no children's data either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
